Meera Pahad: From Social Exclusion to Community Leadership

“From the margins of society to becoming a trusted livestock healthcare provider, Meera’s story is a powerful testament to women’s empowerment through skills, opportunity, and community-based development.”

In Nanda village, located in the challenging terrain of Junnardeo block, 40-year-old Meera Pahad had long experienced social exclusion and discrimination. Despite being educated, Meera and her family faced marginalisation because of their Scheduled Caste identity and were compelled to live on the outskirts of the village, relying on goat-rearing with little recognition or social acceptance.

A turning point came when Meera joined the Pashu Sakhi programme, an initiative by GASVS in partnership with The Goat Trust. Through hands-on veterinary training, Meera gained knowledge and practical skills in identifying common livestock diseases, providing basic treatment, administering vaccinations, and promoting preventive animal healthcare.

In July 2024, when an outbreak of Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD) threatened livestock in Nanda village and put the livelihoods of dozens of families at risk, Meera stepped forward. Her timely clinical response, care, and guidance on disease control contained the outbreak and safeguarded village herds. For the community, longstanding social perceptions changed as Meera's expertise and dedication became undeniable.

Today, Meera earns approximately ₹8,000 per month through her veterinary healthcare services, with her expertise sought across Nanda and neighbouring villages. For Meera, this transformation represents the recovery of personal dignity, equal participation in community life, and respected leadership as a skilled professional.

Solar Sakhi Spotlight Rakhi-Kol Village · Junnardeo Block

Khushi Vishwakarma: Lighting Homes, Building Futures

At 19, Khushi was selected for the intensive residential Solar Engineer Training Programme supported by Bindi International and executed by GASVS. Despite tragically losing her father during her training in Rajasthan, she persevered with steadfast resolve and earned her professional solar engineering certification.

Returning to Junnardeo, Khushi has single-handedly installed clean solar lighting systems in over 100 un-electrified households, earning ₹30,000 from initial installations. Today, she sustains an ongoing monthly income of ~₹5,000 through system maintenance and solar equipment sales, serving as an inspiration for girls across the Satpura belt.

Inclusive Livelihood Spotlight Teegaon Village · Pandhurna District

Sanjay Waghale: From Isolation to Enterprise & Public Leadership

Sanjay Waghale, a 40-year-old man living with a locomotor disability, experienced severe economic dependency. Supported by GASVS under community-based rehabilitation, he secured his UDID certification, government pension, and an entrepreneurship grant to establish a goat-rearing enterprise.

Demonstrating exceptional management acumen, Sanjay expanded his livestock herd and was elected President of his village Self-Help Group. In 2025, his proven leadership and technical dependability resulted in his formal appointment as the official Gram Panchayat Pump Operator, achieving complete financial self-sufficiency.
